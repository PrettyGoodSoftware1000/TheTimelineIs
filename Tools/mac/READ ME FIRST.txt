THE TIMELINE IS — Mac


PUTTING IT ON YOUR MAC

1. Drag "The Timeline Is" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it.


THE FIRST TIME, MACOS WILL REFUSE

You will see something like:

    "The Timeline Is" can't be opened because Apple cannot check it
    for malicious software.

This is not a problem with the game. It means nobody has paid Apple to
vouch for it. You only have to get past it once.

1. Click Done on that message.
2. Open System Settings.
3. Go to Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll down. There is a line about "The Timeline Is" being blocked,
   with an "Open Anyway" button. Click it.
5. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

It opens normally from then on. You never have to do this again.


THE CONTROLS

Move the view       arrows, or WASD, or drag with the right button
Zoom                scroll wheel, or two fingers on a trackpad
Pick someone        click them
Add to the pick     Shift-click
Add or drop one     Command-click
Everyone            Tab
Play a card         click it, or press its number
End your turn       Space
Cancel              right-click, or Escape
See the grid        hold Command

Command does every job Control does on a PC. Control is not used.


WHERE YOUR SAVE LIVES

    ~/Library/Application Support/TheTimelineIs/

Deleting that folder starts you over. Deleting the app leaves it alone.
