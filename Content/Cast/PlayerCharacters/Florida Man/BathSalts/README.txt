Drop .png files in here and Bath Salts will show them.

Two kinds of file:

  Anything.png      one picture. Fades in, hangs there, fades out. Two seconds.

  Gator1.png        a numbered series is ONE animation, not three pictures.
  Gator2.png        Same name, different number. They play in number order at
  Gator3.png        30 frames a second and do not fade between frames.

Numbers are read as numbers, so Gator10.png comes after Gator9.png rather than
after Gator1.png. A lone Gator1.png with no Gator2.png is just a picture.

The order the SHOTS come in is shuffled every time the card is played. The
frames inside a series never are.

Nothing here is required. With the folder empty the card still does everything
else it does - it just says so rather than showing a black screen.
